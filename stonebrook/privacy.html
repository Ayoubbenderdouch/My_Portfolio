Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version: Stonebrook is a single-player game that works offline. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no in-app purchase, no analytics and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Stonebrook is made by Ayoub Benderdouch. For any question about privacy, write to ayoubbenderdouch@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and your settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

Third-party services

The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics and diagnostics are switched off in this game, so the game does not send information about you or your device to Unity or to any other company. The app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) handle downloads under their own privacy policies.

Children

Stonebrook is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data reaches us, there is nothing we could show, correct or delete. If you write to us by e-mail, we use your message only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
