How do I start?

Chief Brooks shows you around when you start a new game. Whenever you are not sure what to do, tap the flag – it always shows your next step. You can watch the tour again any time: Settings › How to play.

What is my goal?

Keep your residents fed, clean and calm. Then the county sends more residents and Stonebrook grows – from 2 residents to 48, in four chapters.

The daily gift

Once a day the county sends you a gift. Come back on following days and it grows – day 7 is the biggest. If you miss a day, the streak starts again at day 1.

Reminders

The game can remind you once a day when your next gift is ready. Switch reminders on or off in Settings › Reminders.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. While the game is closed, your prison waits for you – nothing happens while you are away. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Japanese and Korean. The game starts in your device's language; change it in Settings › Language.
